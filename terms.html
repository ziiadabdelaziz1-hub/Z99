Legal Governance // Production Protocol

Terms of Service & Engagement

Effective Date: September 2026 • Last Updated: September 15, 2026

01. Scope of Production Services

These Terms of Service govern all creative engagements, technical feasibility assessments, and live turnkey executions provided by Z99 Creative Studio.

Our studio specializes in architectural 3D projection mapping, stadium concert visual stage engines (Notch / TouchDesigner), anamorphic 3D LED installations, holographic stages, and bespoke luxury royal wedding scenography worldwide.

02. Technical Site Surveys & Hardware Requirements

Accurate architectural geometry and physical surface fidelity are essential to our 3D projection pipeline. Clients and event producers agree to coordinate:

  • • Physical Venue Access: Timely access for LiDAR scanning, photogrammetry, and optical laser distance measurements.
  • • Power & Rigging Specifications: Adequate three-phase electrical distribution, certified rigging points, and structural load compliance specified in our technical riders.
  • • Environmental Control: Ambient illumination management and weatherproofing for outdoor architectural projection towers.

03. Production Milestones & Creative Approvals

Turnkey visual engineering is executed according to structured milestone sign-offs:

  1. Milestone 1 (Treatment & Storyboard): Narrative visual architecture, thematic moodboards, and audio-reactive pacing.
  2. Milestone 2 (Pre-Visualization): 3D digital twin architectural viewport simulations and camera angles.
  3. Milestone 3 (Final Rendering & On-Site Calibration): 4K/8K uncompressed master renders, media server alignment, and live show control rehearsals.

04. Intellectual Property & Performance Rights

Unless specified under a separate written assignment:

Z99 Creative Studio retains all intellectual property rights in its proprietary shader graphs, procedural 3D animation assets, TouchDesigner network patches, and calibration techniques. Clients receive an exclusive, royalty-free license to project, perform, and broadcast the completed visual sequence for the agreed event dates.

05. Retainers & Production Rescheduling

Because our production pipeline commits dedicated 3D render nodes, media server operators, and technical directors months in advance, confirmed calendar dates are secured via production retainers. Rescheduling requests must be submitted in writing prior to final master render locking.
