// PROTOCOL 01 • SCOPE

Turnkey Scope of Production Services

These Terms of Service govern all creative contracts, technical site evaluations, and live visual productions executed by Z99 Creative Studio.

Our production scope encompasses architectural 3D projection mapping, stadium concert stage visuals (real-time Notch / TouchDesigner rigs), anamorphic 3D LED engines, and custom scenography for bespoke royal palaces and luxury weddings globally.

Standard of Execution Every production is delivered as a turnkey technical system: narrative treatments, 3D animations, media server calibration, projector geometry alignment, and live show control execution.
// PROTOCOL 02 • TECHNICAL RIDERS

Site Surveys, LiDAR Scans & Hardware Rigging

Flawless optical calibration depends on millimeter-accurate architectural surface data. Clients and event venues coordinate with our technical directors to facilitate:

• LiDAR & Laser Scanning: Physical venue access for terrestrial LiDAR scans and high-resolution photogrammetry.
• Power & Rigging: Certified three-phase electrical distribution, load-tested trussing, and environmental control booths as defined in our technical rider.
• Ambient Light Control: Strict blackout windows and ambient architectural lighting cut-offs during projection alignment and live performances.

// PROTOCOL 03 • WORKFLOW

Milestone Sign-Offs & Creative Approvals

To maintain our uncompromised rendering standards, production is executed through three formal milestone gates:

Stage 01: Narrative & Storyboards • Visual architecture, thematic concept moodboards, and musical pacing sign-off.

Stage 02: 3D Pre-Visualization • Digital twin architectural simulation and viewpoint line-of-sight verification.

Stage 03: Master Renders & On-Site Calibration • Uncompressed master render delivery, media server warping, and live technical rehearsals.

// PROTOCOL 04 • RIGHTS

Intellectual Property & Performance Licenses

Z99 Creative Studio retains all rights, title, and ownership in its proprietary TouchDesigner network architectures, procedural shaders, and calibration algorithms.

Clients receive an exclusive, royalty-free performance license to display, broadcast, and document the finalized visual work for the agreed calendar dates and broadcast platforms.

// PROTOCOL 05 • COMMITTED ASSETS

Production Retainers & Calendar Reservation

Because our studio commits specialized hardware rigs, media server technicians, and 3D render farms months in advance, confirmed calendar dates are secured via production retainers.

Rescheduling requests must be provided in writing at least 21 days prior to scheduled on-site rehearsals to allow technical redeployment.

Dedicated Production Rigs Your date is reserved exclusively with dedicated primary and redundant backup media servers on standby.
// PROTOCOL 06 • SAFETY

Weather Contingencies & Force Majeure

For outdoor architectural projection mapping and festival stages, client safety and optical hardware protection are paramount. In events of severe precipitation or wind exceeding certified rigging thresholds, our technical directors execute predefined backup standby sequences until safe operation resumes.
