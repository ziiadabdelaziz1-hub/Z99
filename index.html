// 05. HOW WE WORK

The Z99 Production Pipeline

From initial concept sketches to monumental live spectacle — our proven 6-step creative workflow.

01 CONCEPT & VISION

The Idea

We start with your building and your story. Sketches, references and a creative direction that fits the architecture.

Concept sketches, moodboard & spatial storytelling.
02 ON-SITE LASER SCAN

Reading the Building

We visit at night and measure everything — surface, light, distances, angles. Nothing is guessed.

Crew on site after dark, laser measure & drone survey.
03 COMPUTATIONAL 3D MODEL

Rebuilding It in 3D

The façade is recreated as an exact digital model, so every window, arch and column becomes a canvas.

Sub-millimeter 3D digital twin of the physical structure.
04 MOTION & SOUNDTRACK

Creating the Show

Animation, visual effects and an original soundtrack, designed to move across the real geometry of the building.

Original score choreographed to geometric illusions.
05 HARDWARE DEPLOYMENT

Building the Setup

Projectors, towers, cables and power go in. Then hours of aligning until the image locks perfectly onto the walls.

Projector towers aligned with laser test grids.
06 LIVE MOMENTUM

Showtime

The building disappears and the story takes over.

The crowd watching — pure spectacle and emotional impact.
