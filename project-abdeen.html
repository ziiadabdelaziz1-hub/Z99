Abdeen Palace: Royal Bridal Entrance

A milestone in Middle Eastern architectural projection mapping: transforming the 19th-century royal neoclassical palace facade into a living computational canvas, choreographed to precision with a live orchestral entrance.

The Architectural Challenge

Abdeen Palace is one of Cairo's most venerable royal landmarks. Its neoclassical stone facade presents intricate architectural ornaments, Corinthian columns, arched windows, and deep reliefs. The challenge was twofold: respect the historical integrity of the palace without altering physical surfaces, and calibrate multiple high-lumen projection feeds to eliminate optical distortion across curved balconies and cornices.

3D Pre-Visualization & Orchestral Choreography

Our engineering team created a sub-millimeter 3D wireframe mesh of the entire facade through drone photogrammetry and laser telemetry. Every column, balustrade, and pediment was mapped in virtual space. Visual narratives were keyed frame-by-frame to a 50-piece live symphony orchestra, with visual blooming, golden illumination waves, and cascading classical arches syncing in real-time as the royal bridal procession emerged.

The Visual Transformation

As the bride stepped onto the grand courtyard, the palace stone dissolved into luminous golden embroidery, followed by cascading floral vines and neoclassical illusions that expanded the physical depth of the palace into infinite virtual halls. The production drew acclaim from international dignitaries and redefined royal celebration production in Egypt.

PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS
  • Venue & Location Abdeen Royal Palace, Cairo, Egypt
  • Discipline Architectural 3D Facade Mapping
  • Projected Surface Area Over 1,800 m² of Classical Facade
  • Projection Technology Multi-Laser High-Lumen Stacks (4K Native)
  • Audio Synchronization SMPTE Timecode / Live Symphony Tracking
  • Production Pipeline LiDAR Mesh • Unreal Engine 5 • TouchDesigner
  • Delivery Timeline 4 Weeks from Facade Scan to Live Show
