Fossil: 3D Timepiece CGI Simulation

Crafting photorealistic luxury CGI and cinematic motion design for Fossil: microscopic metallic grain simulation, anisotropic brushed steel reflections, and mechanical kinetic choreography for high-definition retail screens and commercial broadcasting.

01 — 3D Render

02 — Actual Show

The Luxury Commercial Vision

Luxury horology demands perfection down to microscopic tolerances. For Fossil's global timepiece launch, traditional macro cinematography was constrained by depth-of-field limitations and lens artifacts. The brand sought a 100% computational 3D CGI simulation that would celebrate the craftsmanship, internal gear interplay, and tactile allure of the watch face.

Microscopic Shader Engineering

We engineered bespoke physically based materials (PBR) calibrated to match real-world stainless steel, sapphire crystal with anti-reflective coating caustics, and sunray brushed dial finishes. Micro-scratches and chamfered edges were sculpted at sub-millimeter scale to achieve indistinguishable photorealism under intense moving studio key lights.

Kinetic Motion & Screen Deployment

The motion choreography deconstructs the timepiece in zero gravity, showing the rhythmic escapement wheel, ticking hands, and interlocking escapement gears before snapping into place with mechanical finality. Rendered across high-dynamic range color spaces for flagship boutique LED columns and digital billboard takeovers.
